About the symposium

Ideas, evidence, and experiences that advance education

The Scheller College of Business symposium provides a forum to discuss emerging trends in higher education, share innovative teaching practices, and explore the changing role of educators in the age of AI.

At its core, the symposium aims to strengthen collaboration between higher education and industry, creating space for candid dialogue about evolving workforce expectations and the competencies students will need to thrive.

The day brings together keynote perspectives, poster presentations, practical teaching innovations, discussions of Georgia Tech's AI ecosystem, and a cross-institutional panel.

What we'll explore together
